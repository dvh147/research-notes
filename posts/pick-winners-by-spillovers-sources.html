Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Hidden Giants 2022 Guillard, Martin, Mohnen, Thomas & Verhoeven Standing on the Shoulders of Hidden Giants LSE POID/CEP WP (web) PDF Namesake. P-Rank spillover measure; Hidden Giants = 56% of external value, 1.7:1 vs Illusory Giants; field marginal social returns >40% vs ~15%; targeting ≥40% above uniform within country, +30% from OECD coordination.
BSV 2013 Bloom, Schankerman, Van Reenen Identifying Technology Spillovers and Product Market Rivalry Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA9466 The prize: social return ≈55% vs private ≈21% (≥2×). The heterogeneity hinge: smaller firms have lower social returns (niches) → reconsider small-firm credits.
AHStantcheva 2022 Akcigit, Hanley, Stantcheva Optimal Taxation and R&D Policies Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA15445 Optimal subsidy design with heterogeneous, private-info research productivity + spillovers; high-productivity firms get higher returns; nonlinear (declining-at-margin) subsidy ≈ optimum.
AAABK 2018 Acemoglu, Akcigit, Alp, Bloom, Kerr Innovation, Reallocation, and Growth AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20130470 Key tension: uniform incumbent R&D subsidies backfire (keep low-types alive); taxing incumbent operation +1.4% welfare by freeing skilled labor.
Cingano 2025 Cingano, Palomba, Pinotti, Rettore Making Subsidies Work: Rules versus Discretion Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA21319 Rules-beat-discretion (RD on funding cutoff): objective-only −11% cost/job; political-discretion-only +42%. Answers the capture objection.
Criscuolo 2019 Criscuolo, Martin, Overman, Van Reenen Some Causal Effects of an Industrial Policy AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20160034 Targeting margin is decisive: 10pp subsidy → ~10% mfg employment, but only for small firms; large firms pocket money without raising activity.
Myers-Lanahan 2022 Myers, Lanahan Estimating Spillovers from Publicly Funded R&D: Evidence from the US DOE AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20210678 Spillover magnitude (~3 spillover patents per recipient patent) AND leakage caveat (many in different tech areas; only ~60% domestic).

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Jones-Williams 1998 Jones, Williams Measuring the social return to R&D QJE (topecon) 10.1162/003355398555856 Optimal R&D is 2–4× actual (conservative lower bound).
Lucking-Bloom-VR 2019 Lucking, Bloom, Van Reenen Have R&D Spillovers Changed? NBER WP 24622 (web) NBER w24622 Updated social:private ≈ 4× (up from ~3×); gap widening.
König 2022 König, Storesletten, Song, Zilibotti From Imitation to Innovation: Where Is All That Chinese R&D Going? Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA18586 Across-the-board R&D subsidies are non-monotonic — very large ones slow TFP by funding the wrong firms; R&D misallocation is costly.
AHS-V 2021 Akcigit, Hanley, Serrano-Velarde Back to Basics: Basic Research Spillovers, Innovation Policy, and Growth ReStud (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdaa061 Uniform research subsidies over-subsidize applied research (lower spillovers); targeting basic research is welfare-improving.
Atkeson-Burstein 2019 Atkeson, Burstein Aggregate Implications of Innovation Policy JPE (topecon) 10.1086/701823 Uniform innovation subsidies move aggregates only modestly over 20 years (welfare gains can still be meaningful).
Ballester 2006 Ballester, Calvó-Armengol, Zenou Who's Who in Networks. Wanted: The Key Player Econometrica (topecon) 10.1111/j.1468-0262.2006.00709.x Optimal target is the highest-intercentrality node, not the biggest.
Galeotti 2020 Galeotti, Golub, Goyal Targeting Interventions in Networks Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA16173 With complementarities, optimal intervention loads on the most globally connected parts of the network.
ABL 2023 Akcigit, Baslandze, Lotti Connecting to Power: Political Connections, Innovation, and Firm Dynamics Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA18338 The capture risk: connected leaders less innovative; aggregate connection gains don't offset reallocation losses.
Santoleri 2024 Santoleri, Mina, Di Minin, Martelli The Causal Effects of R&D Grants: Evidence from a Regression Discontinuity ReStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest_a_01233 What works is funding (relaxing constraints), not a certifying "quality stamp" without money.
Knott-Posen-Wu 2009 Knott, Posen, Wu Spillover Asymmetry and Why It Matters Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1080.0950 Spillover pools are asymmetric/directional, not a common reservoir — supports heterogeneity.
Zacchia 2020 Zacchia Knowledge Spillovers through Networks of Scientists ReStud (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdz033 A firm's social return depends on its position in the inventor network.
Lane 2025 Lane Manufacturing Revolutions: Industrial Policy and Industrialization in South Korea QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjaf025 Targeted HCI policy expanded targeted sectors, spilled downstream via the I-O network, and persisted after it ended.
