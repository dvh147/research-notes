Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Giummo 2010 Jesse Giummo German employee inventors' compensation records: A window into the returns to patented inventions Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2010.04.004 The sharpest single piece of evidence that realized use-value and renewal value are different objects, only weakly correlated
Fischer 2014 Timo Fischer, Jan Leidinger Testing patent value indicators on directly observed patent value — Ocean Tomo patent auctions Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2013.07.013 Validates standard indicators against actual auction prices; citations and family size are real but explain little variance
Jensen 2011 Paul H. Jensen, Russell Thomson, Jongsay Yong Estimating the patent premium: Evidence from the Australian inventor survey Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.925 Separates the value of the patent right from the value of the invention: patents add ~40–50%
Bessen 2009 James Bessen Estimates of patent rents from firm market value Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2009.09.014 The reconciliation paper: renewal-based and market-value-based aggregate rent estimates correspond well
Bessen 2008 James Bessen The value of US patents by owner and patent characteristics Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2008.02.005 Renewal estimates of US patent value; patent rents ≈ 3% of R&D; citations explain little variance in value
KPSS 2017 Leonid Kogan, Dimitris Papanikolaou, Amit Seru, Noah Stoffman Technological innovation, resource allocation, and growth Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjw040 The stock-market ruler: patent-level private value from the market reaction to grant news. Read in the NBER WP 17769 version.
Arora 2026 Ashish Arora, Sharon Belenzon, Elia Ferracuti, Jay Prakash Nagar Revisiting stock market signals as a lens for patent valuation Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.2025.20570 The stock-market ruler's error is systematic, not classical: negatively correlated with true value, so it understates the best patents and overstates the weakest. Read in the revised NBER WP 33056 (April 2026) version.
GHV 2008 Alfonso Gambardella, Dietmar Harhoff, Bart Verspagen The value of European patents European Management Review (web) 10.1057/emr.2008.10 The headline variance number: four standard indicators explain 2.7% of the variance in surveyed patent value (Table 9, p. 80); mean EUR 3.14m, median EUR 397k, mode EUR 6.4k (Table 8, p. 79)
ACC 2008 Ashish Arora, Marco Ceccagnoli, Wesley M. Cohen R&D and the patent premium International Journal of Industrial Organization 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2007.11.004 Defines the patent premium and separates its unconditional from its conditional value. Read in the NBER WP 9431 (2003) version, whose magnitudes differ from what the published article is cited as reporting — the post quotes only the published abstract's qualitative finding.

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Schankerman 1998 Mark Schankerman How valuable is patent protection? Estimates by technology field RAND Journal of Economics (web) 10.2307/2555817 Renewal model fitted to all French patent applications 1969-1987; patent protection is "a significant, but not the major, source of private returns to inventive activity" (the paper's own words). Read in the NBER WP 3780 version.
Lanjouw 1998 Jean O. Lanjouw Patent protection in the shadow of infringement: Simulation estimations of patent value Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1111/1467-937X.00064 Renewal model adding the cost of enforcing against infringers; aggregate value of protection ≈ 10% of related R&D — but for four technology areas only (computers, textiles, combustion engines, pharmaceuticals), West Germany 1953–1988
HNSV 1999 Dietmar Harhoff, Francis Narin, F.M. Scherer, Katrin Vopel Citation frequency and the value of patented inventions Review of Economics and Statistics (topecon) 10.1162/003465399558265 Founding evidence that citations and surveyed value are positively related
HSV 2003 Dietmar Harhoff, F.M. Scherer, Katrin Vopel Citations, family size, opposition and the value of patent rights Research Policy (web) 10.1016/S0048-7333(02)00124-5 Which indicators predict surveyed value, and how the answer varies by technology field
HJT 2005 Bronwyn H. Hall, Adam Jaffe, Manuel Trajtenberg Market value and patent citations RAND Journal of Economics 36(1): 16-38 (web) https://ideas.repec.org/a/rje/randje/v36y20051p16-38.html An extra citation per patent is associated with ~3% higher market value. The one reference with no Crossref DOI, hence the only one with no PDF; the magnitude is corroborated by Bessen (2008) fn. 18 (2.7% at the mean) and the RePEc abstract.
Torrisi 2016 Salvatore Torrisi, Alfonso Gambardella, Paola Giuri, Dietmar Harhoff, Karin Hoisl, Myriam Mariani Used, blocking and sleeping patents: Empirical evidence from a large-scale inventor survey Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2016.03.021 A large share of patents is neither used internally nor traded — value that no revenue-based ruler can see
Giuri 2007 Paola Giuri et al. (PatVal-EU) Inventors and invention processes in Europe: Results from the PatVal-EU survey Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2007.07.008 The survey infrastructure behind the European value estimates
Odasso 2015 Cristina Odasso, Giuseppe Scellato, Elisa Ughetto Selling patents at auction: an empirical analysis of patent value Industrial and Corporate Change (fieldinnov) 10.1093/icc/dtu015 Second auction-price study; offer price vs closing price, and which indicators predict each
Graham 2018 Stuart J.H. Graham, Alan C. Marco, Amanda F. Myers Patent transactions in the marketplace: Lessons from the USPTO Patent Assignment Dataset Journal of Economics & Management Strategy (subtopecon) 10.1111/jems.12262 Scale and limits of observable patent transactions as a valuation source
Gambardella 2007 Alfonso Gambardella, Paola Giuri, Alessandra Luzzi The market for patents in Europe Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2007.07.006 Transaction costs mean many potential licences never happen, so observed prices are a selected sample
Hall 2010 Bronwyn H. Hall, Megan MacGarvie The private value of software patents Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2010.04.007 Event-study and market-value evidence that the marginal patent right can be worth little even when the technology is valuable
Belenzon 2012 Sharon Belenzon Cumulative innovation and market value: Evidence from patent citations Economic Journal (topecon) 10.1111/j.1468-0297.2011.02470.x Citations are not a scalar quality signal: cites the firm builds on raise value, cites it does not lower it
BGV 1999 Richard Blundell, Rachel Griffith, John Van Reenen Market share, market value and innovation in a panel of British manufacturing firms Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1111/1467-937X.00097 Market-value regressions on innovation counts; the value of an innovation depends on who holds it

Serrano 2010 Carlos J. Serrano The dynamics of the transfer and renewal of patents RAND Journal of Economics (web) 10.1111/j.1756-2171.2010.00117.x Renewal and resale are jointly determined, so renewal-based value is partly a statement about the current owner
Lanjouw 2004 Jean O. Lanjouw, Mark Schankerman Patent quality and research productivity: Measuring innovation with multiple indicators Economic Journal (web) 10.1111/j.1468-0297.2004.00216.x The composite-index response: combine noisy indicators into one latent quality measure
