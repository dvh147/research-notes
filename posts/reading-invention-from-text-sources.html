Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Kelly 2021 Kelly, Papanikolaou, Seru & Taddy Measuring Technological Innovation over the Long Run AER: Insights (web) 10.1257/aeri.20190499 Flagship. Novelty = backward text dissimilarity, impact = forward text similarity; the measure predicts future citations and correlates strongly with market value, and reconstructs technological waves 1836–present (electricity 1880s, chemicals 1920s–30s, computing post-1980). The coverage anvil.
Arts 2021 Arts, Hou & Gomez NLP to identify the creation and impact of new technologies in patent text Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2020.104144 The horse race: text novelty/impact separate award patents (Nobel, Hall of Fame) from triple-status (USPTO-granted, EPO+JPO-rejected) patents better than citation/classification metrics.
Arts 2018 Arts, Cassiman & Gomez Text matching to measure patent similarity SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.2699 Expert-validated text similarity improves on patent-class similarity; replicates the localization-of-spillovers result with text-matched controls (text can carry a real design). Open code/data.
Yang 2025 Yang, Alex J. Text vs. citations: A comparative analysis of breakthrough and disruption metrics Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2025.105295 The head-to-head on 6M+ patents: text (KI) and citation (CD) indices highly correlated but diverge — secular trend, U-shape in future citations, and small/remote teams scoring high on text but low on citations.
Alcacer 2009 Alcácer, Gittelman & Sampat Applicant and examiner citations in US patents Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2008.12.001 The hook fact: examiners add 63% of citations on the average patent and all citations on 40% of patents — so most citations aren't the inventor's.
Roach 2013 Roach & Cohen Lens or Prism? Patent Citations as a Measure of Knowledge Flows from Public Research Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1120.1644 Citations capture codified flows but systematically miss private/contract/basic-research knowledge; using them alone substantially underestimates public science's effect.
Moser 2018 Moser, Ohmstedt & Rhode Patent Citations — An Analysis of Quality Differences and Citing Practices in Hybrid Corn Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2016.2688 The honest counter: against objective field-trial yield performance, citations are robustly correlated with real quality — citations aren't worthless, only differently biased.

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
CriscuoloVerspagen 2008 Criscuolo & Verspagen Does it matter where patent citations come from? Inventor vs. examiner citations in European patents Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2008.07.011 Inventor- and examiner-added citations behave differently; the examiner-share problem recurs in EP data.
Corsino 2019 Corsino, Mariani & Torrisi Firm strategic behavior and the measurement of knowledge flows with patent citations SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.3016 Citation measurement error is systematic — it depends on patent strategy, knowledge type, and jurisdiction (validated against an inventor survey).
Bessen 2008 Bessen The value of US patents by owner and patent characteristics Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2008.02.005 Citations "explain little variance in value" (renewal-based estimates) — a weak guide to importance.
Macher 2024 Macher, Rutzer & Weder Is there a secular decline in disruptive patents? Correcting for measurement bias Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2024.104992 Citation-structure metrics are fragile: 88% of the patent "disruption decline" is a pre-1976 truncation artifact.
Liu 2025 Liu, Amdaoud, Mescheba et al. NPL v. NLP: analysing the links between science and technology with citations and semantics Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-025-05460-6 Citation and text signals of science→tech linkage only partly converge; many citations are weakly relevant (legal/strategic); use full text + both.
Chen 2017 Chen Do patent citations indicate knowledge linkage? Evidence from text similarities J. of Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2016.04.018 Balance: citing–cited pairs are more textually similar than random pairs — a citation does, on average, encode a real knowledge link.
Higham 2021 Higham, de Rassenfosse & Jaffe Patent Quality: Towards a Systematic Framework for Analysis and Measurement Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2021.104215 Every quality proxy (text or citation) is inconsistent across the outcomes you validate against — no measure is "the" truth.
