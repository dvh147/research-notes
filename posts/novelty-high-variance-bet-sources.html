conti2014 Conti (2014) Do non-competition agreements lead firms to pursue risky R&D projects? Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.2155 Closest-to-causal anchor: enforcement nudge pushes inventions into BOTH tails + novel areas (direction only).
singh-fleming2010 Singh & Fleming (2010) Lone inventors as sources of breakthroughs: myth or reality? Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1090.1072 The two tails move independently; collaboration trims failures and fattens breakthroughs (quantile regs).
arts-veugelers2015 Arts & Veugelers (2015) Technology familiarity, recombinant novelty, and breakthrough invention Industrial and Corporate Change (fieldinnov) 10.1093/icc/dtu029 The honest exception: novelty over FAMILIAR components raises mean + breakthroughs AND cuts failure.
arts-fleming2018 Arts & Fleming (2018) Paradise of novelty -- or loss of human capital? Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.2018.1216 Exploring new fields: novelty up, value down; downside muted by expert collaborators / science (FE + 2SLS).
kaplan-vakili2015 Kaplan & Vakili (2015) The double-edged sword of recombination in breakthrough innovation Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.2294 Novelty != value: cognitive novelty (local search) vs economic value (broad recombination); only 20/189 do both.
