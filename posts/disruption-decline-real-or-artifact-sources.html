Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Park 2023 Park, Leahey & Funk Papers and patents are becoming less disruptive over time Nature (web) 10.1038/s41586-022-05543-x The headline finding + magnitudes (91.9–100% papers; 78.7–91.5% patents); its own robustness checks, non-CD linguistic corroboration, and the "conservation of highly disruptive work."
Petersen 2024 Petersen, Arroyave & Pammolli The disruption index is biased by citation inflation Quant. Science Studies (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1162/qss_a_00333 The central magnitude critique: CD mechanically falls as reference lists grow.
Petersen 2025 Petersen, Arroyave & Pammolli The disruption index suffers from citation inflation: Re-analysis of temporal CD trend and team size J. of Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2024.101605 Temporal re-analysis; PNAS-vs-PNAS-Plus quasi-experiment; disruption rises 2005–2015.
Macher 2024 Macher, Rutzer & Weder Is there a secular decline in disruptive patents? Correcting for measurement bias Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2024.104992 The patent-leg demolition: 88% of the decline is a pre-1976 truncation artifact; corrected, disruptive patents rise since 2008.
LeibelBornmann 2024 Leibel & Bornmann What do we know about the disruption index? An overview Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-023-04873-5 Authoritative review of the index's biases and variants; "trust the direction, not the decimals."
BornmannTekles 2021 Bornmann & Tekles Convergent validity of disruptiveness indicators vs expert milestones J. of Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2021.101159 The specific index version used in the headline studies is not the most valid; DI5 aligns better with experts.
Bloom 2020 Bloom, Jones, Van Reenen & Webb Are Ideas Getting Harder to Find? American Economic Review (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20180338 Independent (non-CD) evidence of falling research productivity — the macro backdrop.
ChuEvans 2021 Chu & Evans Slowed canonical progress in large fields of science PNAS (web) 10.1073/pnas.2021636118 Independent mechanism: field-size → canonical ossification (rank corr 0.25→0.74), no CD index involved.

BornmannFunk 2025 Bornmann & Funk Popper's probability calculus and the decline of scientific disruptiveness Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-025-05404-0 A coauthor of the original paper offers a theoretical account of a real decline.
KluppelKnott 2023 Klüppel & Knott Are ideas being fished out? Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2022.104665 Counter-nuance: the slowdown is NOT idea-exhaustion (production rises in the knowledge stock).
