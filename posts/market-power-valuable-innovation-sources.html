Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI How used
Martin 2025 Martin, Solorzano Mosquera, Thomas & Verhoeven Firm markups and the economic value of innovation IJIO (field IO) 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2025.103186 The anchor: high-markup firms' innovation is more valuable privately AND externally.
DLEU 2020 De Loecker, Eeckhout & Unger The Rise of Market Power and the Macroeconomic Implications QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjz041 Hook: markups 21%->61%, profit 1%->8%; the "markups are rising/bad" backdrop.
ABBGH 2005 Aghion, Bloom, Blundell, Griffith & Howitt Competition and innovation: An inverted-U relationship QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/120.2.701 Theory is two-sided: inverted-U between competition and innovation.
BGV 1999 Blundell, Griffith & Van Reenen Market share, market value and innovation in a panel of British manufacturing firms Rev. Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1111/1467-937X.00097 Firm-level precursor: market share -> more innovation; value reward larger for high-share firms.
CEM 2021 Cunningham, Ederer & Ma Killer Acquisitions JPE (topecon) 10.1086/712506 Objection: market power destroys invention; 5.3-7.4% of acquisitions are "killer".
De Ridder 2024 De Ridder Market Power and Innovation in the Intangible Economy AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20201079 Aggregate counter: intangibles -> high markups + entry deterrence -> fading dynamism.
LMS 2022 Liu, Mian & Sufi Low Interest Rates, Market Power, and Productivity Growth Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA17408 Aggregate counter: leaders pull ahead, followers discouraged, growth slows.
ABBKL 2023 Aghion, Bergeaud, Boppart, Klenow & Li A Theory of Falling Growth and Rising Rents Rev. Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdad016 Reconciles firm-level strength with aggregate slowdown (dynamic, non-monotone).

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI Point it supports
ADKPV 2020 Autor, Dorn, Katz, Patterson & Van Reenen The Fall of the Labor Share and the Rise of Superstar Firms QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjaa004 High-markup firms are productive "superstars," not sleepy monopolists; concentration rose where productivity grew fastest.
EMX 2023 Edmond, Midrigan & Xu How Costly Are Markups? JPE (topecon) 10.1086/722986 The consumer-side welfare cost of markups is real and large.
HP 2012 Hottenrott & Peters Innovative Capability and Financing Constraints for Innovation: More Money, More Innovation? Rev. Economics & Statistics (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00227 Deep-pockets mechanism: financing constraints bind on innovative firms; internal funds -> more innovation.
Bighelli 2023 Bighelli, di Mauro, Melitz & Mertens European Firm Concentration and Aggregate Productivity JEEA (subtopecon) 10.1093/jeea/jvac040 Caveat: rising European concentration can reflect efficiency, with markups roughly flat.
DLW 2012 De Loecker & Warzynski Markups and Firm-Level Export Status AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.102.6.2437 Measurement: the production-function approach to estimating firm markups (used by the anchor).
HJT 2005 Hall, Jaffe & Trajtenberg Market Value and Patent Citations RAND J. of Economics (field IO) 10.1111/j.1756-2171.2005.tb00073.x Measurement: citations <-> firm value (extra citation/patent ~ +3% value); private-value proxy.
