Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI How used
JTH 1993 Jaffe, Trajtenberg & Henderson Geographic Localization of Knowledge Spillovers as Evidenced by Patent Citations QJE (topecon) 10.2307/2118401 The foundational result: citations are ~6% same-metro vs ~1% expected; the matched-control method; fades slowly.
Alcácer & Gittelman 2006 Alcácer & Gittelman Patent Citations as a Measure of Knowledge Flows: The Influence of Examiner Citations REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest.88.4.774 The contamination critique: ⅔ of citations are examiner-added; 40% of patents all-examiner.
Thompson–Fox-Kean 2005 Thompson & Fox-Kean Patent Citations and the Geography of Knowledge Spillovers: A Reassessment AER (topecon) 10.1257/0002828053828509 The control-matching critique: with finer controls, state/metro localization largely disappears.
Thompson 2006 Thompson Patent Citations and the Geography of Knowledge Spillovers: Evidence from Inventor- and Examiner-added Citations REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest.88.2.383 Within-patent rebuttal: inventor citations 20% (country)/25% (state-metro) more local than examiner.
Murata et al. 2014 Murata, Nakajima, Okamoto & Tamura Localized Knowledge Spillovers and Patent Citations: A Distance-Based Approach REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00422 The methodological resolution: distance-based test finds localization for most tech classes even with fine controls.
Ganguli, Lin & Reynolds 2020 Ganguli, Lin & Reynolds The Paper Trail of Knowledge Spillovers: Evidence from Patent Interferences AEJ: Applied (subtopecon) 10.1257/app.20180017 Non-citation design: interfering inventors 1.4–4.0× more likely same local area; more concentrated than citation-linked.
Bai, Jin & Zhou 2024 Bai, Jin & Zhou Proximity and Knowledge Spillovers: Evidence from the Introduction of New Airline Routes Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2021.01717 Causal design: 20% travel-time cut → 0.5% more knowledge flow (>15,000 citations), via inventor flow / tacit knowledge.
Almeida & Kogut 1999 Almeida & Kogut Localization of Knowledge and the Mobility of Engineers in Regional Networks Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.45.7.905 The mechanism: inter-firm engineer mobility drives local knowledge transfer (esp. Silicon Valley).
Singh & Marx 2013 Singh & Marx Geographic Constraints on Knowledge Spillovers: Political Borders vs. Spatial Proximity Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1120.1700 Borders beyond distance: state/country borders have independent effects; state effect waning, country strengthening.
Belenzon & Schankerman 2013 Belenzon & Schankerman Spreading the Word: Geography, Policy, and Knowledge Spillovers REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00334 Localization is institutional: citations stop at state lines; border effect bigger where non-competes strong (Michigan reform).

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI Point it supports
Henderson, Jaffe & Trajtenberg 2005 Henderson, Jaffe & Trajtenberg Patent Citations and the Geography of Knowledge Spillovers: A Reassessment: Comment AER (topecon) 10.1257/0002828053828644 The original authors' reply to Thompson–Fox-Kean (fine matching discards data).
Arts, Cassiman & Gomez 2018 Arts, Cassiman & Gomez Text Matching to Measure Patent Similarity SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.2699 Localization replicates with text-matched (not classification-based) controls.
Verluise et al. 2025 Verluise, Cristelli, Higham & de Rassenfosse Beyond the Front Page: In-Text Citations to Patents as Traces of Inventor Knowledge SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.70027 Inventor-origin (in-text) citations show even stronger geographic proximity.
Myers & Lanahan 2022 Myers & Lanahan Estimating Spillovers from Publicly Funded R&D: Evidence from the US Department of Energy AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20210678 Spillovers are large (≈3 per grant patent) and ~60% stay within the US.
Singh & Agrawal 2011 Singh & Agrawal Recruiting for Ideas: How Firms Exploit the Prior Inventions of New Hires Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1100.1253 Hiring raises use of recruit's prior inventions ~219%, but spillover to others is narrow and local.
Giuri & Mariani 2013 Giuri & Mariani When Distance Disappears: Inventors, Education, and the Locus of Knowledge Spillovers REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00259 The hedge: highly educated inventors access geographically wider spillovers.
Kerr 2008 Kerr Ethnic Scientific Communities and International Technology Diffusion REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest.90.3.518 Networks carry knowledge across borders (ethnic scientific communities).
Zacchia 2020 Zacchia Knowledge Spillovers through Networks of Scientists Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdz033 Spillovers travel through co-authorship networks, not only space.
Chattergoon & Kerr 2022 Chattergoon & Kerr Winner Takes All? Tech Clusters, Population Centers, and the Spatial Transformation of US Invention Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2021.104418 The stakes: US invention increasingly concentrated in tech hubs.
