Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
HHJK 2019 Hsieh, Hurst, Jones, Klenow The Allocation of Talent and US Economic Growth Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA11427 The lens; macro anchor — 20–40% of US growth/person 1960–2010 from better talent allocation; 94%→62% white-male doctors/lawyers.
Bell-QJE 2019 Bell, Chetty, Jaravel, Petkova, Van Reenen Who Becomes an Inventor in America? QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjy028 Entry margin — top-1% kids 10× likelier to invent; environment not ability; "innovation rate would quadruple."
Bell-JEEA 2019 Bell, Chetty, Jaravel, Petkova, Van Reenen Do Tax Cuts Produce More Einsteins? JEEA (subtopecon) 10.1093/jeea/jvz013 Entry margin — top 1% of inventors earn >22% of inventor income; incentives "limited," exposure matters.
Çelik 2023 Murat Alp Çelik Does the Cream Always Rise to the Top? The Misallocation of Talent in Innovation J. Monetary Economics (web/WP) WP PDF Entry margin — meritocratic allocation raises innovation ~10% (model, WP version).
MSF 2009 Marx, Strumsky, Fleming Mobility, Skills, and the Michigan Non-Compete Experiment Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1080.0985 Matching margin — enforcement cut inventor mobility 8.1%, +15.4 / +16.2% more for firm-specific/narrow specialists; patenting rate did NOT fall (honesty point).
MSF 2015 Marx, Singh, Fleming Regional disadvantage? Non-compete agreements and brain drain Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2014.10.006 Matching margin — non-compete brain drain, concentrated in highest-impact & most collaborative inventors.
AG 2023 Akcigit, Goldschlag Where Have All the "Creative Talents" Gone? NBER WP 31085 (web) nber.org/w31085 Matching margin — inventors absorbed by incumbents (share 49%→57%, 2000–16); incumbent hire → earnings +12.6%, output −6 to −11%.
Bisset–Verhoeven 2024 Bisset, Verhoeven Intellectual Mobility Frictions Working paper (web) SSRN 5302724 Matching margin (seed) — easing field-mobility frictions could raise idea generation ~50% (model).
Prato 2024 Marta Prato The Global Race for Talent QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjae040 Upside — migrant inventors +33%/yr; origin co-inventors +16%/yr; doubling H-1B +4% growth via sorting.
ACG 2016 Akcigit, Çelik, Greenwood Buy, Keep, or Sell: Economic Growth and the Market for Ideas Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA12144 Upside — market for ideas; ~16% of patents sold, mismatched 13% likelier to sell; growth ladder 2.1%→3.4%.
APP 2024 Akcigit, Pearce, Prato Tapping into Talent Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdae047 Upside/policy — education subsidy +20% innovation vs R&D subsidy +5% for equal money.
