Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Moretti 2025 Moretti, Steinwender & Van Reenen The Intellectual Spoils of War? Review of Economics and Statistics (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest_a_01293 The central modern causal estimate: 10% more govt R&D -> +4.3% private R&D, productivity gains, international spillovers
Gross-Sampat 2023 Gross & Sampat America, Jump-Started American Economic Review (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20221365 The WWII OSRD natural experiment: +40-50% cluster patenting by 1970; permanent redirection of US invention
Azoulay 2019 Azoulay, Graff Zivin, Li & Sampat Public R&D Investments and Private-sector Patenting: Evidence from NIH Funding Rules Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdy034 Cleanest "spills beyond funded firms": $10M NIH -> ~2.7 private-sector patents, no crowd-out elsewhere
Goolsbee 1998 Goolsbee Does Government R&D Policy Mainly Benefit Scientists and Engineers? American Economic Review P&P (web) NBER w6532 The key objection: inelastic scientist supply -> wage inflation; R&D-policy effectiveness estimates 30-50% too high
Wallsten 2000 Wallsten The Effects of Government-Industry R&D Programs on Private R&D: The Case of SBIR RAND Journal of Economics (web) 10.2307/2601030 Additionality objection: SBIR grants crowd out firm R&D ~dollar-for-dollar; program design matters
David-Hall-Toole 2000 David, Hall & Toole Is Public R&D a Complement or Substitute for Private R&D? A Review of the Econometric Evidence Research Policy (web) 10.1016/S0048-7333(99)00087-6 The pre-2000 prior was genuinely mixed; frames the post as "the verdict shifted with identification"

Bergeaud 2025 Bergeaud, Guillouzouic, Henry & Malgouyres From Public Labs to Private Firms: Magnitude and Channels of Local R&D Spillovers Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjaf034 Academic research crowds in private R&D (EUR1 -> EUR0.81), raises patent quality; channels = mobility, firm creation, partnerships
Akcigit 2021 Akcigit, Hanley & Serrano-Velarde Back to Basics: Basic Research Spillovers, Innovation Policy, and Growth Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdaa061 Basic research has the largest spillovers; public basic research is welfare-improving (the niche private R&D underfunds)
Bloom-Schankerman-Van Reenen 2013 Bloom, Schankerman & Van Reenen Identifying Technology Spillovers and Product Market Rivalry Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA9466 Social returns to R&D ~2x private - the spillover wedge that makes crowd-in socially valuable
Toole 2012 Toole The impact of public basic research on industrial innovation: Evidence from the pharmaceutical industry Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2011.06.004 Public basic research feeds private (pharma) innovation - the spillover channel in a second domain
Howell 2025 Howell, Rathje, Van Reenen & Wong Opening Up Military Innovation: Causal Effects of Reforms to US Defense Research Journal of Political Economy (topecon) 10.1086/737235 Design of defense research matters: broadening access had real innovation effects ("not a blank check")
