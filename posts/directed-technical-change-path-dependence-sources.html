Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI How used
Aghion 2016 Aghion, Dechezleprêtre, Hemous, Martin & Van Reenen Carbon Taxes, Path Dependency, and Directed Technical Change JPE (topecon) 10.1086/684581 The workhorse: clean/dirty patent elasticities to fuel prices (~+1.0 / −0.6); own-stock path-dependence elasticities (0.31 clean, 0.56 dirty); +40% price → clean overtakes in ~15 yrs; "double-edged sword."
AABH 2012 Acemoglu, Aghion, Bursztyn & Hemous The Environment and Directed Technical Change AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.102.1.131 The theory: temporary policy permanently redirects iff clean & dirty are strong substitutes; cost of delay; carbon tax + research subsidy mix.
AAGHK 2016 Acemoglu, Akcigit, Hanley & Kerr Transition to Clean Technology JPE (topecon) 10.1086/684511 Calibrated optimal policy: immediate + temporary, front-loaded R&D subsidy + hump-shaped carbon tax, both withdrawn; 50-yr delay ≈ 300-yr switch; tax-alone must rise >3×.
Calel-Dechezleprêtre 2016 Calel & Dechezleprêtre Environmental Policy and Directed Technological Change: Evidence from the European Carbon Market REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00470 Real-world test: EU ETS raised regulated firms' low-carbon patenting ~36% (matched), no crowd-out, but only ~1% of EU total; persistence untested.
Hanlon 2015 Hanlon Necessity Is the Mother of Invention: Input Supplies and Directed Technical Change Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA10811 Cleanest historical natural experiment: US Civil War cotton shock → ~3× rise in Indian-cotton-augmenting patents; strong induced bias (price rebound).
Popp 2002 Popp Induced Innovation and Energy Prices AER (topecon) 10.1257/000282802760015658 Energy-patent price elasticity ≈ 0.35; AND the objection: diminishing returns to a technology's knowledge stock (induced bursts fade within a technology).
Newell-Jaffe-Stavins 1999 Newell, Jaffe & Stavins The Induced Innovation Hypothesis and Energy-Saving Technological Change QJE (topecon) 10.1162/003355399556188 Appliance efficiency: ~¼–½ of post-1973 efficiency gains were price-induced; direction of change responds to prices.

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI Point it supports
Acemoglu 2002 Acemoglu Directed Technical Change Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1111/1467-937X.00226 The modern foundation: innovation flows toward what is profitable to improve (market-size + price effects).
Papageorgiou 2017 Papageorgiou, Saam & Schulte Substitution between Clean and Dirty Energy Inputs: A Macroeconomic Perspective REStat (subtopecon) 10.1162/REST_a_00592 The pivotal real-world parameter: clean–dirty energy elasticity of substitution > 1 (~1.8 electricity; 1.4–2.9 industry) — the condition the temporary-policy result needs.
Moscona-Sastry 2023 Moscona & Sastry Does Directed Innovation Mitigate Climate Damage? Evidence from US Agriculture QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjac039 Convergent domain: US agricultural innovation redirects toward climate-affected crops (partial offset).
San 2023 San Labor Supply and Directed Technical Change: Evidence from the Termination of the Bracero Program in 1964 AEJ: Applied (fieldothers) 10.1257/app.20200664 Convergent domain: cutting farm labor induced labor-saving mechanization — direction responds to input supply.
