Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Wu 2019 Wu, Wang & Evans Large teams develop and small teams disrupt Nature (web) 10.1038/s41586-019-0941-9 The headline pattern + magnitudes (70/30/50%); within-author + Nobel validation.
FunkOS 2017 Funk & Owen-Smith A Dynamic Network Measure of Technological Change Mgmt Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2015.2366 Defines the consolidate/disrupt (CD) index — the ruler everything rides on.
Park 2023 Park, Leahey & Funk Papers and patents are becoming less disruptive over time Nature (web) 10.1038/s41586-022-05543-x The same ruler over time (91.9-100% papers; 78.7-91.5% patents) — the cautionary mirror.
Petersen 2024 Petersen, Arroyave & Pammolli The disruption index is biased by citation inflation Quant. Science Studies (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1162/qss_a_00333 Citation-inflation critique of the decline.
Petersen 2025 Petersen, Arroyave & Pammolli The disruption index suffers from citation inflation: re-analysis ... team size J. of Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2024.101605 The strongest objection: team-size link weak/non-monotonic, flips positive at >=8.
LinLiWu 2025 Lin, Li & Wu Team size and its negative impact on the disruption index J. of Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2025.101678 Rebuttal: team-size effect holds with longer (>=10yr) citation windows.
LeeWalshWang 2015 Lee, Walsh & Wang Creativity in scientific teams: unpacking novelty and impact Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2014.10.007 Independent corroboration via novelty (inverted-U) — doesn't use the CD index.
Jones 2009 Jones The Burden of Knowledge and the "Death of the Renaissance Man" Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1111/j.1467-937X.2008.00531.x Why teams grew: specialization + teamwork as the frontier advances.
OsorioBornmann 2021 Osorio & Bornmann On the disruptive power of small-teams research Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-020-03756-3 The selection mechanism: disruptive ideas choose small teams.
