Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Ahmadpoor-Jones 2017 Ahmadpoor & Jones The dual frontier Science (web) 10.1126/science.aam9527 Measurability of distance-to-science; 80%/61% linkage; importance concentrates at the boundary.
Krieger-Schnitzer-Watzinger 2024 Krieger, Schnitzer & Watzinger Standing on the shoulders of science SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.3598 The value gradient: 5–18% within-firm / up to ~40% across-firm premium; monotonic decay with distance; tails & novelty (working-paper magnitudes).
Poege et al. 2019 Poege, Harhoff, Gaessler & Baruffaldi Science Quality and the Value of Inventions Science Advances (web) 10.1126/sciadv.aay7323 Cited-science quality prices the patent (top-science patents ~2× value); strong selection; 3-yr window rules out reverse causation.
Gittelman-Kogut 2003 Gittelman & Kogut Does good science lead to valuable knowledge? Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.49.4.366.14420 The central tension: important science is negatively linked to top innovations within firms — "good science ≠ useful science."
Fleming-Sorenson 2004 Fleming & Sorenson Science as a map in technological search SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.384 Mechanism: science maps the search landscape, prunes dead ends; most valuable for highly-coupled (breakthrough-prone) problems.
Arora-Belenzon-Patacconi 2018 Arora, Belenzon & Patacconi The decline of science in corporate R&D SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.2693 Countercurrent: corporate science (and its market value) declining 1980–2006, even as science stays useful as an input.
Arora-Belenzon-Dionisi 2023 Arora, Belenzon & Dionisi First-mover advantage and the private value of public science Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2023.104867 Key qualifier: private returns to public science are small except for first-movers — appropriability, not pure science.
Mansfield 1991 Mansfield Academic research and industrial innovation Research Policy (web) 10.1016/0048-7333(91)90080-A Seminal quantification: 11%/9% of products/processes, 14% of radical breakthroughs; ~7-yr lag; ~28% (25%) social return.

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Narin et al. 1997 Narin, Hamilton & Olivastro The increasing linkage between U.S. technology and public science Research Policy (web) 10.1016/S0048-7333(97)00013-9 73% of patent-cited papers are public science; founding linkage result.
Marx-Fuegi 2020 Marx & Fuegi Reliance on science: worldwide front-page patent citations to scientific articles SMJ (topman) 10.1002/smj.3145 The open dataset that made distance-to-science computable at scale; citations to science growing.
Marx-Fuegi 2022 Marx & Fuegi Reliance on science by inventors: hybrid extraction of in-text patent-to-article citations J. Economics & Management Strategy (fieldIO) 10.1111/jems.12455 In-text (body) patent-to-paper citations companion dataset.
Ke 2020 Ke An analysis of the evolution of science-technology linkage in biomedicine J. Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2020.101074 Patent→science citations doubling every ~2.9 years; universities >half of cited science.
Meyer 2010 Meyer Can applied science be 'good science'? Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-009-0154-3 Patent-cited papers far more likely highly cited (13.8%/23.5% vs 4% baseline).
Toole 2012 Toole The impact of public basic research on industrial innovation (pharma) Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2011.06.004 NIH basic research raises new-drug entry at the earliest stage; positive social return (causal-ish).
Nagar et al. 2024 Nagar, Breschi & Fosfuri ERC science and invention: does ERC break free from the EU paradox? Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2024.105038 ERC-funded science → more patent citations and higher-quality citing patents (quasi-experimental).
Simeth-Cincera 2016 Simeth & Cincera Corporate Science, Innovation, and Firm Value Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2015.2220 Firms that publish science have higher market value — the flip side of the corporate-science retreat.
Wu et al. 2024 Wu, Xie & Du Does science disrupt technology? Scientometrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1007/s11192-024-05126-9 Science-referencing patents are more disruptive; effect growing — but recency is negatively related to disruptiveness (caveat on "recent").
Buchmann-Wolf 2024 Buchmann & Wolf Breakthrough inventions in solar PV and wind technologies: the role of scientific discoveries Technological Forecasting & Social Change (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.techfore.2023.122975 Clean-tech breakthroughs lean disproportionately on prior science.
Meyer 2000 Meyer Does science push technology? Patents citing scientific literature Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/S0048-7333(99)00040-2 Measurement caveat: a patent-paper citation often is not a direct knowledge link.
Wang-Verberne 2024 Wang & Verberne Comparing patent in-text and front-page references to science J. Informetrics (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1016/j.joi.2024.101564 Measurement caveat: front-page vs in-text references barely overlap; value conclusions can flip.
