Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Marx 2009 Marx, Strumsky & Fleming Mobility, Skills, and the Michigan Non-Compete Experiment Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1080.0985 Causal anchor for Link 1: Michigan's 1985 switch cut inventor mobility ~8%, more for specialists.
JLP 2023 Johnson, Lipsitz & Pei Innovation, Inventor Mobility, and the Enforceability of Noncompete Agreements NBER WP 31487 (web) w31487 Headline + Link 3: avg-sized enforceability increase → ~13% less patenting, ~22% less mobility; resolves the trade-off net-negative.
Almeida-Kogut 1999 Almeida & Kogut Localization of knowledge and the mobility of engineers in regional networks Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.45.7.905 Foundational Link 2: knowledge spillovers are localized and ride on engineer mobility (esp. Silicon Valley).
Singh-Agrawal 2011 Singh & Agrawal Recruiting for Ideas: How Firms Exploit the Prior Inventions of New Hires Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1100.1253 Link 2 magnitude: hiring an inventor raises use of her prior inventions ~219%, but spillover is concentrated.
Samila-Sorenson 2011 Samila & Sorenson Noncompete Covenants: Incentives to Innovate or Impediments to Growth Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1100.1280 Link 3: VC produces more patents/startups/jobs in states that restrict non-competes.
Contigiani 2018 Contigiani, Hsu & Barankay Trade secrets and innovation: Evidence from the "inevitable disclosure" doctrine Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.2941 Link 3: strengthening employer-friendly trade secrecy lowers affected inventors' patenting.
Marx-Singh-Fleming 2015 Marx, Singh & Fleming Regional disadvantage? Employee non-compete agreements and brain drain Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2014.10.006 Link 3: enforcement drives a brain drain to non-enforcing states, concentrated among top inventors.
Shi 2023 Shi Optimal Regulation of Noncompete Contracts Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA18128 Objection 1 / welfare: structural model finds non-compete rent extraction socially excessive; optimal ≈ ban.
Conti 2014 Conti Do non-competition agreements lead firms to pursue risky R&D projects? Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.2155 Counter-evidence: enforcement pushes firms toward riskier, more novel R&D (appropriability upside).

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Starr-Frake-Agarwal 2019 Starr, Frake & Agarwal Mobility Constraint Externalities Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.2018.1252 Non-competes lower offers/mobility/wages even for workers who never signed.
Lipsitz-Starr 2022 Lipsitz & Starr Low-Wage Workers and the Enforceability of Noncompete Agreements Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2020.3918 Oregon's 2008 ban raised job-to-job mobility and wages (2–3%; up to 14–21% for the bound).
Song-Almeida-Wu 2003 Song, Almeida & Wu Learning-by-hiring: When is mobility more likely to facilitate interfirm knowledge transfer? Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.49.4.351.14429 Mobility transfers knowledge most when the hire's expertise is distant from the new firm.
Corredoira-Rosenkopf 2010 Corredoira & Rosenkopf Should auld acquaintance be forgot? The reverse transfer of knowledge through mobility ties Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.803 Mobility-driven knowledge flows are bidirectional, especially across distance.
Chen-Balasubramanian-Forman 2024 Chen, Balasubramanian & Forman How does worker mobility affect business adoption of a new technology? Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.3595 Investment channel: easier mobility lowers firms' costly tech (ML) adoption.
Melero-Palomeras-Wehrheim 2020 Melero, Palomeras & Wehrheim The Effect of Patent Protection on Inventor Mobility Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2019.3500 Non-competes aren't the only lock-in: +1 patent → ~23% less likely to move.
Kang-Lee 2022 Kang & Lee How innovating firms manage knowledge leakage: A natural experiment on the threat of worker departure Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.3404 Firms adapt: higher departure threat → more patenting (disclosure) vs. secrecy.
Seo-Somaya 2022 Seo & Somaya Living It Up at the Hotel California: Employee Mobility Barriers and Collaborativeness in Firms' Innovation Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.2021.1461 Firms adapt: mobility barriers push them to collaborate more internally.
Cheyre-Klepper-Veloso 2015 Cheyre, Klepper & Veloso Spinoffs and the Mobility of US Merchant Semiconductor Inventors Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2014.1956 Caveat to the legend: Silicon Valley mobility was driven by spinoffs, not only the ban.
Starr-Bala-Sakakibara 2018 Starr, Balasubramanian & Sakakibara Screening Spinouts? How Noncompete Enforceability Affects the Creation, Growth, and Survival of New Firms Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2016.2614 Nuance: enforcement cuts the number of within-industry spinouts but raises their quality.
Starr-Prescott-Bishara 2021 Starr, Prescott & Bishara Noncompete Agreements in the US Labor Force Journal of Law and Economics (web) 10.1086/712206 Prevalence (~18% bound, ~38% ever signed) and that non-competes bind even where unenforceable.
