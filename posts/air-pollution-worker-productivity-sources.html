GraffZivinNeidell2012 Graff Zivin & Neidell (2012) Pollution & worker productivity (fruit pickers, ozone) AER (topecon); also seed 10.1257/aer.102.7.3652 Founding causal evidence, physical channel: 10 ppb ozone drop → +4.2% productivity, below federal limits.
Chang2019CallCenter Chang, Graff Zivin, Gross & Neidell (2019) Pollution & call-center workers AEJ: Applied (subtopecon); also seed 10.1257/app.20160436 Service/white-collar productivity: +10 AQI → −0.35% calls, via longer breaks.

Ebenstein2016Exam Ebenstein, Lavy & Roth (2016) Long-run consequences of high-stakes exams (pollution) AEJ: Applied (subtopecon) 10.1257/app.20150213 Cognition with durable stakes: +1 SD exam-day PM2.5 → −1.7% SD score; later attainment & earnings.
HoffmannRud2024 Hoffmann & Rud (2024) The Unequal Effects of Pollution on Labor Supply Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA20484 Labor-supply margin + distribution: −7.5% hours on extreme days; poor cut hours less (avoidance is a luxury).
FuViardZhang2021 Fu, Viard & Zhang (2021) Air Pollution & Manufacturing Firm Productivity (China) Economic Journal (subtopecon) 10.1093/ej/ueab033 Scales micro→macro: 1 µg/m³ → +0.82% TFP; 1% national PM2.5 cut → +0.039% GDP.
BorgschulteMolitorZou2024 Borgschulte, Molitor & Zou (2024) Air Pollution & the Labor Market: Wildfire Smoke Rev. Economics & Statistics (subtopecon) 10.1162/rest_a_01243 US/rich-country external validity + welfare: +1 smoke day → −0.1% quarterly earnings; welfare ≈ mortality cost.
