Core papers (anchor an argument)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Hager 2024 Hager, Schwarz & Waldinger Measuring Science AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20230515 The spine: natural experiment in citation visibility → assortative matching, hidden stars, Matthew effects. Analyzed in depth (full text).
Hsieh 2019 Hsieh, Hurst, Jones & Klenow The Allocation of Talent and US Economic Growth Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA11427 Stakes: 20-40% of US output-per-person growth 1960-2010 from improved talent allocation.
Bell 2019 Bell, Chetty, Jaravel, Petkova & Van Reenen Who Becomes an Inventor in America? QJE (web) 10.1093/qje/qjy028 "Lost Einsteins": top-1% kids ~10x more likely to invent; the overlooked are disproportionately women/minorities/poor.
Heller-Kessler 2024 Heller & Kessler Information Frictions and Skill Signaling in the Youth Labor Market AEJ: Economic Policy (subtopecon) 10.1257/pol.20220544 Mechanism analogy: a credible quality signal (rec letters) raised youth employment +3pp.
Bernstein 2026 Bernstein, Frydman & Hilt The Value of Ratings: Evidence from their Introduction in Securities Markets Journal of Political Economy 134(3) (web) 10.1086/739333 Mechanism analogy: Moody's 1909 bond ratings (no legal force, public info) cut bid-ask spreads, re-priced bonds.
Koffi 2025 Marlène Koffi Innovative Ideas and Gender (In)equality American Economic Review 115(7) (web) 10.1257/aer.20211811 Why women are hidden: citation-tracing omits female-authored papers 15-20% more; women need ~20 pct more innovativeness for equal cites.
Ross 2022 Ross, Glennon, Murciano-Goroff, Berkes, Weinberg & Lane Women are credited less in science than men Nature 608 (web) 10.1038/s41586-022-04966-w Cleanest hidden-figures magnitude: women 13% less likely named as authors, 59% less on patents, for work they did.
Bol 2018 Bol, de Vaan & van de Rijt The Matthew effect in science funding PNAS 115(19) (web) 10.1073/pnas.1719557115 The Matthew counterweight, causal (RD): early funding win → >2x later funding (+EUR 180k/8yr), +47% full-prof odds (28 vs 19%).

Supporting papers (cited for a specific point)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
MSV 1991 Murphy, Shleifer & Vishny The Allocation of Talent: Implications for Growth QJE (web) 10.2307/2937945 Seminal: where a society's ablest people go determines growth.
Azoulay 2010 Azoulay, Graff Zivin & Wang Superstar Extinction QJE (topecon) 10.1162/qjec.2010.125.2.549 Collaborators measurably shape output: a star coauthor's unexpected death cuts collaborators' output 5-8% → who you work with matters, so matching matters.
Azoulay 2019 Azoulay, Fons-Rosen & Graff Zivin Does Science Advance One Funeral at a Time? AER (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20161574 Incumbency/gatekeeping: fields open to outsiders only after a star dies — the cousin of the Matthew worry.
Card 2022 Card, DellaVigna, Funk & Iriberri Gender Differences in Peer Recognition by Economists Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA18027 Women historically under-recognized relative to their records.
Sarsons 2021 Sarsons, Gerxhani, Reuben & Schram Gender Differences in Recognition for Group Work JPE (topecon) 10.1086/711401 Women get less credit for co-authored work — a reason their quality is "hidden."
Agrawal 2016 Agrawal, Lacetera & Lyons Does standardized information in online markets disproportionately benefit job applicants from less developed countries? J. International Economics (fieldothers) 10.1016/j.jinteco.2016.08.003 A standardized/verified quality signal helps exactly the overlooked applicants.
Garfield 1955 Eugene Garfield Citation Indexes for Science Science 122(3159) (web) 10.1126/science.122.3159.108 The founding proposal for citation indexing.
Hirsch 2005 Jorge E. Hirsch An index to quantify an individual's scientific research output (the h-index) PNAS 102(46) (web) 10.1073/pnas.0507655102 The metric machinery the modern backlash reacts to.
Merton 1968 Robert K. Merton The Matthew Effect in Science Science 159(3810) (web) 10.1126/science.159.3810.56 Names the cumulative-advantage mechanism (the honest catch).
Strathern 1997 Marilyn Strathern 'Improving ratings': audit in the British University system European Review 5(3) (web) 10.1017/S1062798700002660 Source of the Goodhart restatement: "when a measure becomes a target..."
