Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Koning 2021 Koning, Samila & Ferguson Who do we invent for? Science (web) 10.1126/science.aba6990 Headline: all-female biomedical teams 35% more likely than all-male to focus on women's health (1976–2010); "thousands of missing female-focused inventions."
Koning 2020 Koning, Samila & Ferguson Inventor Gender and the Direction of Invention AEA P&P (web) 10.1257/pandp.20201045 Identification crux: 10pp more female inventors → 1.2pp more female-focused patents, only for female-led teams.
Einiö, Feng & Jaravel 2023 Einiö, Feng & Jaravel Social Push and the Direction of Innovation VATT WP 160 / CEP DP1861 (web) SSRN 3383703 Generalizes: inventors make products bought by people like them (gender/age/income), within & across industries; female inventors +2.2pp / +3.9% female-headed-household sales.
Acemoglu & Linn 2004 Acemoglu & Linn Market Size in Innovation: Theory and Evidence from the Pharmaceutical Industry QJE (topecon) 10.1162/0033553041502144 The demand-side foil: potential market size (US demographics) raises drug entry, robust to supply controls.
Hunt 2013 Hunt, Garant, Herman & Munroe Why are women underrepresented amongst patentees? Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2012.11.004 The "few women invent" half: women = 5.5% of commercialized-patent holders; gap concentrated among S&E-degree holders.
Bhaskarabhatla 2021 Bhaskarabhatla, Cabral, Hegde & Peeters Are Inventors or Firms the Engines of Innovation? Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2020.3646 Mechanism: inventor human capital is 5–10× more important than firm capabilities — the direction travels with the person.
Oksenberg & Zehavi 2025 Oksenberg & Zehavi Consumption targeted innovation for women: From principle to practice? Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2024.105132 Policy upshot + independent market-failure framing ("designs that cater to male needs … ignoring those of women").

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Dubois 2015 Dubois, de Mouzon, Scott-Morton & Seabright Market Size and Pharmaceutical Innovation RAND J Econ (fieldIO) 10.1111/1756-2171.12113 Demand-pull magnitude: elasticity ≈ 0.23; ~$2.5B revenue per new chemical entity.
Blume-Kohout & Sood 2013 Blume-Kohout & Sood Market size and innovation: Effects of Medicare Part D on pharmaceutical R&D J Public Econ (fieldothers) 10.1016/j.jpubeco.2012.10.003 Part D raised R&D in therapeutic classes seniors use most.
Costinot 2019 Costinot, Donaldson, Kyle & Williams The More We Die, the More We Sell? A Simple Test of the Home-Market Effect QJE (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjz003 Demographics → demand → which drugs get invented and sold (demand directs direction).
Mendonça & Reis 2020 Mendonça & Reis Exploring the mechanisms of gender effects in user innovation TFSC (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.techfore.2020.119988 User-innovation channel: gender differences are in who innovates, less in how.
