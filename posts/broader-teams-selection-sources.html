Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Verhoeven 2021 Verhoeven, D. Knowledge Diversity in Teams and Innovation SSRN / AOM (web) SSRN 3795476 Central new evidence: inventor-death design → no causal effect of breadth; correlation is positive selection.
Lee 2010 Lee, J. Heterogeneity, Brokerage, and Innovative Performance Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.1090.0488 Brokerage–performance link vanishes under inventor fixed effects → selection.
Hernandez, Lee & Shaver 2025 Hernandez, E.; Lee, J.K.; Shaver, J.M. Toward an improved causal test of network effects Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.3679 Structural-hole effect only for self-driven change → "spuriously captures unobservable firm characteristics."
Poege et al 2025 Poege, F.; Gaessler, F.; Hoisl, K.; Harhoff, D.; Dorner, M. Filling the Gap: The Consequences of Collaborator Loss in Corporate R&D Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2022.03045 845 inventor deaths; internal collaborator loss ≈ 0 → breadth fungible inside the firm.
Horwitz & Horwitz 2007 Horwitz, S.K.; Horwitz, I.B. The Effects of Team Diversity on Team Outcomes: A Meta-Analytic Review Journal of Management (web) 10.1177/0149206307308587 Meta: task-related diversity modestly positive; bio-demographic ≈ null; effects small.
van Dijk et al 2012 van Dijk, H.; van Engen, M.L.; van Knippenberg, D. Defying conventional wisdom: demographic vs job-related diversity Org. Behavior & Human Decision Processes (web) ScienceDirect S0749597812000751 · 10.1016/j.obhdp.2012.06.003 Meta (146 studies, 612 effects): job-related benefit partly rater bias; fades with objective measures.
Hoisl, Gruber & Conti 2017 Hoisl, K.; Gruber, M.; Conti, A. R&D team diversity and performance in hypercompetitive environments Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.2577 Inverse-U: diversity helps then hurts via coordination costs.
Genin et al 2023 Genin, A.; Ma, W.; Bhagwat, V.; Bernile, G. Board experiential diversity and corporate radical innovation Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.3499 Counterweight: IV + DiD → experiential diversity raises radical innovation (kind, board level).

Ancona & Caldwell 1992 Ancona, D.G.; Caldwell, D.F. Demography and Design: Predictors of New Product Team Performance Organization Science (topman) 10.1287/orsc.3.3.321 Functional diversity aids process but net effect on performance is negative.

Singh & Fleming 2010 Singh, J.; Fleming, L. Lone Inventors as Sources of Breakthroughs: Myth or Reality? Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.1090.1072 Collaboration/experience diversity reshapes the tails (trims failures) more than the mean.
Wu, Wang & Evans 2019 Wu, L.; Wang, D.; Evans, J.A. Large teams develop and small teams disrupt science and technology Nature (web) 10.1038/s41586-019-0941-9 Team structure changes the kind of work (disrupt vs develop), not just the count.
Vakili & Kaplan 2021 Vakili, K.; Kaplan, S. Organizing for innovation: A contingency view on innovative team configuration Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.3264 Optimal breadth-vs-depth configuration depends on the technology — no single recipe.
