Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Anckaert 2025 Paul-Emmanuel Anckaert When the drugs (don't) work: The role of science in product commercialization Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2025.105237 The spine. 5,613 early-stage drug candidates 1995-2008 linked to their underlying publications; basic science alone does not predict market approval, the basic-plus-applied combination does, most strongly for novel drugs and when the firm supplies its own applied insight
FlemingSorenson 2004 Lee Fleming, Olav Sorenson Science as a map in technological search Strategic Management Journal (topman) 10.1002/smj.384 The prior the post pushes against, and the source of the boundary condition it exploits: science helps most where components are tightly coupled
ScannellBosley 2016 Jack W. Scannell, Jim Bosley When Quality Beats Quantity: Decision Theory, Drug Discovery, and the Reproducibility Crisis PLoS ONE 11(2), e0147215 (web) 10.1371/journal.pone.0147215 The mechanism, as a decision-theoretic model: an absolute 0.1 change in the correlation between a screening model's output and clinical utility beats a tenfold throughput gain
Gittelman 2016 Michelle Gittelman The revolution re-visited: Clinical and genetics research paradigms and the productivity paradox in drug discovery Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2016.01.007 The historical version of the mechanism: clinical discovery learns from patient feedback, the genetics paradigm defers it; the switch was institutional, not evidence-driven
AliGittelman 2016 Ayfer Ali, Michelle Gittelman Research paradigms and useful inventions in medicine: Patents and licensing by teams of clinical and basic scientists in Academic Medical Centers Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2016.03.015 The counterweight: MD teams license more than PhD teams, but mixed MD-PhD teams do not. Two academic medical centres, 30 years, hazard models of licensing
HaeusslerAssmus 2021 Carolin Haeussler, Anne Assmus Bridging the gap between invention and innovation: Increasing success rates in publicly and industry-funded clinical trials Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2020.104155 Independent support at the level of people: 3,889 clinical trials; balanced basic-plus-applied skill in the lead investigator raises success, breadth across disease fields lowers it
AnckaertCassiman 2020 Paul-Emmanuel Anckaert, David Cassiman, Bruno Cassiman Fostering practice-oriented and use-inspired science in biomedical research Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2019.103900 The supply side: clinical scientists' basic research is more use-inspired than matched articles, and a fellowship releasing them from clinical duties shifts output toward bench-and-bedside science
AroraBelenzonSheer 2021 Ashish Arora, Sharon Belenzon, Lia Sheer Knowledge Spillovers and Corporate Investment in Scientific Research American Economic Review (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20171742 The rival explanation for the corporate retreat from science: 800,000 corporate publications 1980-2015; firms research more when they use it internally, less when rivals do
