Versions read

Four of the seven full texts obtained are pre-publication versions, which matters only where a magnitude is quoted:

  • Jones & Williams (1998) — Stanford / Federal Reserve Board working paper. It says optimal R&D is "more than four times" actual; the published QJE abstract says "at least two to four times", and the post uses the published range.
  • Bloom, Schankerman & Van Reenen (2013) — LSE Research Online accepted version. The 58% / 20.8% figures are from p. 32 of that version.
  • Comin (2004) — NBER working paper 10625; wording of the headline bound matches the published Journal of Economic Growth abstract.
  • Goldin et al. (2024) — MPRA version; consulted only, not cited in the post.

Akcigit et al. (2021) was recovered on a second pass as the authors' own copy (accepted version, 7 May 2020, 79pp) — no DOI route reaches it, only a title search.

Not obtained: Mansfield et al. (1977) and Arora et al. (2021). Both are genuinely closed — Unpaywall reports no open version, and JSTOR, Oxford Academic and the AEA all refuse automated PDF requests even through an authenticated library proxy. Only Mansfield is cited in the post, on abstract-level evidence and with no quoted number.
