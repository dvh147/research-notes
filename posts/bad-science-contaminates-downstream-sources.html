Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI How used
lu-2013 (seed) Lu, Jin, Uzzi, Jones (2013) The Retraction Penalty Scientific Reports (web) 10.1038/srep03146 Ring 1: author's own prior work loses ~7%/yr, growing to 12.5% at 5+ yrs; reaches 4 network degrees; self-report shields.
scandal-2017 (seed) Azoulay, Bonatti, Krieger (2017) The career effects of scandal Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2017.07.003 Ring 1: ~11% discount on author's other work; 18% (misconduct) vs 8% (mistake); eminence penalty only under misconduct.
guilt-2019 Hussinger & Pellens (2019) Guilt by association: How scientific misconduct harms prior collaborators Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2018.01.012 Ring 2: uninvolved prior collaborators lose ~9% citations; stigma (output unaffected); regressive.
reverse-matthew-2019 Jin, Jones, Lu, Uzzi (2019) The Reverse Matthew Effect Review of Economics and Statistics (subtopecon; web-verified) 10.1162/rest_a_00780 Ring 2: within-team penalty borne by juniors (avg -11pp), eminent shielded (+3pp/SD).
retractions-2015 Azoulay, Furman, Krieger, Murray (2015) Retractions Review of Economics and Statistics (subtopecon; web-verified) 10.1162/REST_a_00469 Ring 3: related non-retracted neighbors lose 5-10%; new papers & funding into field decline.
serra-gneezy-2021 Serra-Garcia & Gneezy (2021) Nonreplicable publications are cited more than replicable ones Science Advances (web) 10.1126/sciadv.abd1705 Hook + persistence: non-replicable cited ~16 more/yr; gap persists post-failure; only ~12% of later cites note the failure.
hsiao-schneider-2022 Hsiao & Schneider (2022) Continued use of retracted papers Quantitative Science Studies (fieldbibliometrics) 10.1162/qss_a_00155 Persistence: only 5.4% of post-retraction citations acknowledge the retraction.
star-wars-2016 Brodeur, Le, Sangnier, Zylberberg (2016) Star Wars: The Empirics Strike Back AEJ: Applied (subtopecon) 10.1257/app.20150044 Base rate: 10-20% of marginally-significant elite-journal results look misallocated (p-hacking).
