Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
BSV 2013 Bloom, Schankerman & Van Reenen Identifying Technology Spillovers and Product Market Rivalry Econometrica (topecon) 10.3982/ECTA9466 The two opposing externalities (knowledge spillover + / business-stealing -); spillover dominates so gross social returns are "at least twice" private (point estimates ~60% vs ~20%); smaller firms generate lower social returns.
ArquéCastells-Spulber 2022 Arqué-Castells & Spulber Measuring the Private and Social Returns to R&D: Unintended Spillovers versus Technology Markets Journal of Political Economy (topecon) 10.1086/719908 The central objection: adding voluntary technology transfers (markets for technology) shrinks the measured social-private gap; inventors are compensated more than the spillovers-only view implies.
Jones-Williams 1998 Jones & Williams Measuring the social return to R&D Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1162/003355398555856 Maps return estimates into underinvestment: optimal R&D is at least 2-4x actual, a conservative lower bound.
Mansfield 1977 Mansfield, Rapoport, Romeo, Wagner & Beardsley Social and Private Rates of Return from Industrial Innovations Quarterly Journal of Economics 10.2307/1885667 web 17 innovations: median social return 56% vs private 25%; ~30% privately unprofitable yet socially worthwhile; private-social rank correlation not significantly different from zero (small n). Read directly from Table II (p.233-234).
Nordhaus 2004 William D. Nordhaus Schumpeterian Profits in the American Economy: Theory and Measurement NBER WP 10433 nber.org/papers/w10433 web The appropriability mechanism + the hook: producers captured only ~2.2% of the social surplus from innovation, 1948-2001 (instantaneous appropriability ~7%).
KPSS 2017 Kogan, Papanikolaou, Seru & Stoffman Technological Innovation, Resource Allocation, and Growth Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjw040 web Dollar private value of patents from stock-price reactions is positively but imperfectly related to citations (social/technological proxy); value distribution extremely skewed.

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Lucking et al. 2018 Lucking, Bloom & Van Reenen Have R&D Spillovers Changed? NBER WP 24622 nber.org/papers/w24622 web Updating BSV to 2015, the social-to-private return ratio rises to ~4 (from ~3); no evidence the divergence has narrowed.
Jones-Summers 2020 Jones & Summers A Calculation of the Social Returns to Innovation NBER WP 27863 nber.org/papers/w27863 web Macro accounting: even conservatively, >= ~$4 of social value per $1 of R&D (the stakes).
Belenzon 2012 Sharon Belenzon Cumulative Innovation and Market Value: Evidence from Patent Citations Economic Journal (subtopecon) 10.1111/j.1468-0297.2011.02470.x The market rewards a firm mainly for follow-on it can internalize, far less for citations from other firms; it prices what the firm keeps, not what it gives away.
AbramsAkcigitGrennan 2013 Abrams, Akcigit & Grennan Patent Value and Citations: Creative Destruction or Strategic Disruption? NBER WP 19647 nber.org/papers/w19647 web Using patent-licensing (NPE) revenue, the value-citation link is an inverted-U: the most valuable patents are cited less (strategic patents). Specific population - treated as illustration, not law.
Gaessler et al. 2025 Gaessler, Harhoff, Sorg & von Graevenitz Patents, Freedom to Operate, and Follow-on Innovation: Evidence from Post-Grant Opposition Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2019.02294 When a patent is revoked in post-grant opposition, follow-on innovation by other firms rises - the private right had suppressed socially valuable follow-on.
Azoulay et al. 2019 Azoulay, Graff Zivin, Li & Sampat Public R&D Investments and Private-sector Patenting: Evidence from NIH Funding Rules Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdy034 Policy upshot: a $10M boost in NIH funding yields ~2.7 additional private-sector patents, some at firms that never received a grant.
Gambardella 2023 Alfonso Gambardella Private and social functions of patents: Innovation, markets, and new firms Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2023.104806 Conceptual frame: patents serve distinct private and social functions (protection + organizing markets/new firms).
