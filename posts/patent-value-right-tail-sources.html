Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Scherer-Harhoff 2000 Scherer & Harhoff Technology policy for a world of skew-distributed outcomes Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/S0048-7333(99)00089-X The canonical skew magnitude (top 10% = 48–93% of returns); the policy inversion and non-diversifiability.
HNSV 1999 Harhoff, Narin, Scherer & Vopel Citation frequency and the value of patented inventions Review of Economics and Statistics (subtopecon) 10.1162/003465399558265 Survey value of German full-term patents; a citation near the median ≈ >$1M; citations track value.
Kogan et al. 2017 Kogan, Papanikolaou, Seru & Stoffman Technological Innovation, Resource Allocation, and Growth Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjw040 Modern large-sample anchor: 1.8M patents, "fat-tailed", median $3.2M (1982$); Amazon/IBM examples; citation–value link.
Schankerman 1998 Schankerman How valuable is patent protection? Estimates by technology field RAND Journal of Economics (fieldindustrialorganization) 10.2307/2555817 Renewal-model evidence the distribution is "highly skewed"; patents a minor part of returns to R&D; varies by field.
Giuri et al. 2007 Giuri, Mariani, Brusoni, Crespi, Gambardella et al. Inventors and invention processes in Europe: Results from the PatVal-EU survey Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2007.07.008 Inventor-survey method; ~68% of European patents worth < €1M (the crowded left wall).

(The two seed papers above are also analyzed in depth: HSV 2003 supplies the top-10%≈80–90% share and the citation/family-size predictors; Bessen 2008 supplies the renewal-based mean $78k vs median $7k and "citations explain little variance.")

Supporting papers (cited for specific points)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL Point it supports
Gambardella-Harhoff-Verspagen 2008 Gambardella, Harhoff & Verspagen The Value of European Patents European Management Review (web) 10.1057/emr.2008.10 Mean >€3M, median ~€300k, mode a few-thousand €; 10% of patents ≈ 80% of value.
Silverberg-Verspagen 2007 Silverberg & Verspagen The size distribution of innovations revisited: extreme value statistics applied to patent significance Journal of Econometrics (web) ScienceDirect The upper tail is Pareto (power-law), heavier than lognormal — the "how heavy is the tail" debate.
Torrisi et al. 2016 Torrisi, Gambardella, Giuri, Harhoff, Hoisl & Mariani Used, blocking and sleeping patents: evidence from a large-scale inventor survey Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2016.03.021 Many patents are never used or licensed ("sleeping"/"blocking") — the median patent earns little.
Fischer-Leidinger 2014 Fischer & Leidinger Testing patent value indicators on directly observed patent value — Ocean Tomo auctions Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2013.07.013 Real auction prices: citations/family size predict value but explain only a small share of variance.
Hall-Jaffe-Trajtenberg 2005 Hall, Jaffe & Trajtenberg Market value and patent citations RAND Journal of Economics (fieldindustrialorganization) IDEAS/RePEc Extra citation ≈ +3% market value — citations correlate with value (but don't determine it).
Bessen 2009 Bessen Estimates of patent rents from firm market value Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2009.09.014 Tobin's-Q cross-check that renewal-based estimates line up with market-value estimates.
Gambardella 2013 Gambardella The economic value of patented inventions: thoughts and some open questions Int'l J of Industrial Organization (fieldindustrialorganization) 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2013.02.007 Framing review: value of patent rights vs value via the number of inventions.

Background / seminal lineage

Key Authors (year) Title Source DOI / URL Why included
Pakes 1986 Pakes Patents as Options: Some Estimates of the Value of Holding European Patent Stocks Econometrica (cited generically; full ref via Schankerman 1998 / Kogan 2017) Origin of the renewal-as-financial-option valuation method.
Schankerman & Pakes 1986 Schankerman & Pakes Estimates of the Value of Patent Rights in European Countries During the Post-1950 Period Economic Journal (cited generically; full ref via Schankerman 1998) Early renewal-based value estimates; lineage for the option logic.
