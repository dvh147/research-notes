Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
Azoulay 2019 Azoulay, Graff Zivin, Li & Sampat Public R&D Investments and Private-sector Patenting Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdy034 The anchor causal estimate ($10M NIH → ~2.3–2.7 private patents); "academic patenting only a small fraction"; private value $0.70–$2.13 per $1. Also a seed.
Myers-Lanahan 2022 Myers & Lanahan Estimating Spillovers from Publicly Funded R&D: Evidence from the US DOE American Economic Review (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20210678 The "untreated firms" magnitude: ~3 spillover patents per recipient patent; recipients capture only 25–50% of value; 60% domestic.
Babina 2023 Babina, He, Howell, Perlman & Staudt Cutting the Innovation Engine Quarterly Journal of Economics (topecon) 10.1093/qje/qjac046 The central objection: funding cuts raise (low-quality, privately assigned) patenting while cutting basic publications and entrepreneurship. Also a seed.
Akcigit 2021 Akcigit, Hanley & Serrano-Velarde Back to Basics: Basic Research Spillovers, Innovation Policy, and Growth Review of Economic Studies (topecon) 10.1093/restud/rdaa061 The policy logic: basic-research spillovers are large; uniform subsidies oversubsidize applied research; public basic research is welfare-improving.
Toole 2012 Toole The impact of public basic research on industrial innovation: Evidence from the pharmaceutical industry Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2011.06.004 The pharma lineage and lag: NIH basic research → new-drug entry at the earliest discovery stage; positive social return.
Arora 2021 Arora, Belenzon & Sheer Knowledge Spillovers and Corporate Investment in Scientific Research American Economic Review (topecon) 10.1257/aer.20171742 The appropriability micro-foundation: firms do less research when rivals can use it; US corporate science is retreating — the gap public funding fills.
