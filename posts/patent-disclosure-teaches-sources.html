Core papers (analyzed in depth)

Key Authors (year) Title Journal (tier) DOI / URL How used
HHZ 2023 Hegde, Herkenhoff & Zhu Patent Publication and Innovation JPE (topecon) 10.1086/723636 Heavyweight: AIPA twin-DiD; +14.7% follow-on cites @10y, 25-29% faster, -5.2% abandonments, +3.95% R&D. The "publish sooner" anchor.
FNW 2021 Furman, Nagler & Watzinger Disclosure and Subsequent Innovation (PTDL) AEJ:EP (subtopecon) 10.1257/pol.20180636 Access channel: library opening -> +8-20% local patenting; effect vanishes post-internet. The "make it reachable" anchor.
BarSim 2020 Baruffaldi & Simeth The Effect of Early Disclosure Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2020.103927 AIPA RDD; ~+12% forward cites per year-faster; "absorptive capacity"/attorney-firm nuance.
Gross 2023 Gross, Daniel P. The Hidden Costs of Securing Innovation: Compulsory Invention Secrecy Management Science (topman) 10.1287/mnsc.2022.4457 The mirror: WWII secrecy orders suppress follow-on; words diffuse only after 1945 lift.
dRPR 2024 de Rassenfosse, Pellegrino & Raiteri Do patents enable disclosure? Evidence from the invention secrecy act Int'l J Industrial Organization (fieldindustrialorganization) 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2023.103044 The mirror (cleanest): secrecy cuts follow-on ~40%, ~45% "lost generation" after lift; distance gradient.
BFR 2022 Buttner, Firat & Raiteri Patents and Knowledge Diffusion: The Impact of Machine Translation Research Policy (fieldinnov) 10.1016/j.respol.2022.104584 Legibility channel: MT of Chinese patents -> +7-10% US cites; pharma flips negative.
